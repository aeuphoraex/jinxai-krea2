HOW IT WORKS
------------
1. Wear the Velvet HUD (touch it to open the menu).
2. Top up your wallet once, in L$, from the HUD.
3. Pick a performer, say what you want, and order.
4. Your picture is rendered for your avatar — not pulled off a shelf.
5. The HUD gives you a link to your own page when it is finished.
